### Objectives

- **O1**: A person who opens `mythhelm run` or `review` on a dark or a light terminal sees the Observatory or the Paper theme matching it, can override the choice with one flag, and sees no change in plain, accessible or JSONL output.
- **O2**: At every width and height rung the first lines answer what is happening (header and NOW), what happens next (NEXT) and what needs the person (key bar slot), in the same order.
- **O3**: `q`, `d`, `s` and Ctrl-C do what their labels say: detach leaves the worker running, stop shows "stop requested" until the worker confirms, and neither is a silent cancel.
- **O4**: Budget and evidence values show their provenance (reported, estimated, observed, user-declared) and show `unknown` as visible text, never blank or zero.
- **O5**: Colour only reinforces text and symbols, every text-bearing colour pair passes a checked contrast ratio, and the four new glyphs have distinct ASCII fallbacks.
