### Objectives

- **O1**: A person who opens `mythhelm run` or `review` on a dark or a light terminal sees the Observatory or the Paper theme matching it, can override the choice with one flag, and sees no change in plain, accessible or JSONL output.
- **O2**: At every width and height rung the first lines answer what is happening (header and NOW), what happens next (NEXT) and what needs the person (key bar slot), in the same order.
- **O3**: `q`, `d`, `s` and Ctrl-C do what their labels say: detach leaves the worker running, stop shows "stop requested" until the worker confirms, and neither is a silent cancel.
- **O4**: Budget and evidence values show their provenance (reported, estimated, observed, user-declared) and show `unknown` as visible text, never blank or zero.
- **O5**: Colour only reinforces text and symbols, every text-bearing colour pair passes a checked contrast ratio, and the four new glyphs have distinct ASCII fallbacks.

### FR-1 — Brand themes and their accessibility check (v2 §15.1, §16; I11, I13; G09, G08)

- **AC-1.1** [v2 §15.1] The built-in `dark` theme shall carry the Observatory tokens (surface `#090f1a`, surface_raised `#0f1a2b`, text `#ebeff5`, text_muted `#989fab`, focus `#4ddae9`, attention `#f0c27b`, ok `#7fd8a9`, err `#f4918a`, border `#4b5668`) and the built-in `light` theme shall carry the Paper tokens (surface `#f6f4ee`, surface_raised `#ebe8df`, text `#101621`, text_muted `#59616f`, focus `#00829d`, attention `#8a5a00`, ok `#23794a`, err `#b4232a`, border `#a3a7ae`). A test reads both through `theme.BuiltIn` and asserts every value.
- **AC-1.2** [v2 §16] The theme format shall carry the border as a validated `#rrggbb` colour in the existing `border` key, and today's `rounded|ascii` style values shall be rejected (OQ-2, default (a)). The format shall keep rejecting unknown keys and shall carry no code path.
- **AC-1.3** [I11] If a theme file holds a non-hex border, an unknown key or a missing required key, then `theme.Load` and `BuiltIn` shall return an error wrapping `ErrInvalidTheme` that names the key. Each case has a kept failing fixture.
- **AC-1.4** [v2 §15.1, AT-36, G09] The theme validator shall check, for each theme, `text`, `text_muted`, `attention` and `err` at 4.5:1 or more on both `surface` and `surface_raised`; `ok` at 4.5:1 or more on `surface`; and `focus` at 3:1 or more on both surfaces. A theme that fails any pair shall be rejected with the pair named (`key on surface contrast N below M`). Kept failing fixtures: a Paper variant with `attention` lightened to 3.0:1 and a variant with `focus` at 2.0:1. The brief's Paper `ok` is 4.39:1 on `surface_raised`, which is why `ok` is checked on `surface` only and AC-1.5 keeps it off overlay cards.
- **AC-1.5** [v2 §15.1, D-97] The renderer shall draw `ok` only on `surface`, and shall draw `focus` only on a single marker cell, a rule or an underline, never as the foreground colour of a text run longer than one glyph cell (the docs spec's choice for the same 4.08:1 pair). The four sites that colour text with `focus` today shall change: the focused pane title (`internal/tui/mission.go:163`), the focused dialog field and button row (`internal/tui/dialogs.go:245`), the selected palette row (`internal/tui/palette.go:133`) and the diff hunk header (`internal/tui/diff.go:511`); each shall render its text in `text` with a `focus`-coloured marker and, for the focused pane title, an underline, and the hunk header shall take `text_muted` plus its `@@` marker. A test parses the SGR runs of full-colour goldens in both themes (the default view at 160x40, the palette, the apply dialog and a diff with a hunk header) and asserts every run coloured `focus` is one marker cell or an underlined run, and that no run coloured `ok` sits in an overlay card. Red against current code at all four sites.
- **AC-1.6** [v2 §15.1] The renderer shall draw `surface_raised` and the border colour on overlay cards (FR-5) and shall use no other background. Whether `surface` itself is painted is OQ-4.
- **AC-1.7** [D-97, v2 §15.1] Amber (`attention`) shall be used only for "needs you": the attention slot of the key bar (AC-4.8) and nothing else. Every other previously `attention`-coloured element (notices, the outcome row, the file boundary in diffs; `internal/tui/live.go:147`, `internal/tui/mission.go:452`, `internal/tui/motion.go:348`, `internal/tui/diff.go:507`) shall change to `text` or `text_muted` plus a symbol or label. Star cyan (`focus`) shall mark mission, focus and working only. The `warning` token is OQ-3.
- **AC-1.8** [v2 §15.1] While `--colour never` or `NO_COLOR` is set, the renderer shall emit no SGR colour sequence and shall carry every state by text and symbol (AC-3.4).

### FR-2 — Light-terminal detection and theme selection (v2 §15.1; I13)

- **AC-2.1** [v2 §15.1] When the launch resolves to the TUI, colour is not suppressed and `--theme auto` (the default), the system shall select Paper when the terminal's background is detected light and Observatory otherwise.
- **AC-2.2** [v2 §15.1, §15.3] The `run`, `demo` and `review` verbs shall accept `--theme auto|dark|light`; `dark` and `light` shall bypass detection and win over it. Any other value shall exit 2 naming `--theme` and the value, on every launch branch including piped output (as `caps.Parse` does for the other three modes, `internal/tui/caps/caps.go:98-118`). Built-in theme ids stay `dark` and `light`; Observatory and Paper are their display names (OQ-2 may revisit).
- **AC-2.3** [v2 §15.1, Doherty] The system shall run its own bounded background query (termenv's constant 5 s wait cannot be bounded). When the terminal's answer has not arrived within the bound, the system shall stop waiting, shall decide from `COLORFGBG` if it is set and says light, and otherwise shall select Observatory, then start the view. The bound is 150 ms (OQ-17 default). A test injects a responder that never answers, with the bound passed in as a parameter, and asserts the decision and that the program starts after it; a second case answers `COLORFGBG=0;15` (light) with no query answer and asserts Paper. No assertion measures wall-clock time.
- **AC-2.4** [v2 §15.1, I13] The system shall not write a background query to the terminal, and shall not read the terminal for one, in any of seven cases: `--plain`, `--accessible`, `--format jsonl`, stdout not a terminal, `TERM=dumb`, `NO_COLOR` set, and `--colour never`. A recording writer asserts zero bytes written for each of the seven cases, and a TTY case asserts the query is written once.
- **AC-2.5** [v2 §15.1] The palette's Toggle theme shall still switch between the two themes after launch, and the chosen theme shall survive a resize and an overlay open and close.
- **AC-2.6** [v2 §15.1] If detection is unavailable on the platform, then `--theme auto` shall select Observatory and `--help` for the flag shall say so (no silent degrade, `.claude/rules/go-conventions.md` Cross-platform). Detection shall not be described as qualified on any terminal until slice 4's matrix exists.
- **AC-2.7** [v2 §15.1] If the terminal's answer arrives after the bound, then the system shall discard it and it shall produce no key event: the late bytes are drained before the input reader starts. A test feeds a realistically shaped late answer (`\x1b]11;rgb:dddd/0000/0000\x1b\\`, whose bytes include `:`, `/`, `d`, `r`, `g` and `b`) and asserts no palette, filter or dialog opens and no action runs.

### FR-3 — Four new glyphs with ASCII fallbacks (D-97, v2 §15.1; I13)

- **AC-3.1** [D-97, v2 §15.1, I13] The closed glyph set in `internal/tui/styles.go` shall gain ◆ (U+25C6), ○ (U+25CB), ◌ (U+25CC) and ✓ (U+2713), each with an ASCII fallback chosen by `--icons ascii` and `TERM=dumb`. The fallbacks are single cells, differ from each other and from the eight existing fallbacks (`- | > * | + - -`), and are OQ-5 (default `#`, `o`, `.`, `v`).
- **AC-3.2** [I13] The font-dependence test (`TestNoFontDependentGlyphs`, `internal/tui/model_test.go:672`) shall be amended so `inPrivateUseOrEmoji` (`:742`) exempts exactly U+2713 and `inGeometricScope` (`:757-768`) admits exactly ◆ ○ ◌ ✓ beside today's ranges, and so a unicode golden contains no non-ASCII rune outside that set and the ASCII golden of every rung contains no byte of 0x80 or above. Kept failing cases: a golden carrying U+2714 or U+2705 fails the test.

## Open Questions

Each question names what it blocks and the options; the default is what `/spec` assumes if nobody answers. Questions that need the maintainer are marked **MAINTAINER**.

1. **OQ-1 — What does Ctrl-C do in the live TUI? MAINTAINER.** Blocks AC-6.5. `specs/*/windows-process-ownership/` AC-3.1 (todo) already pins the foreground Ctrl-C as stop on the first press with exit 130 once confirmed, so (b) would also reopen that spec. 2. **OQ-2 — Theme schema for the border.** Blocks AC-1.2. Options: (a) `border` becomes a `#rrggbb` colour and the unused `rounded|ascii` style goes away (the shape then follows `--icons`); (b) a new `border_colour` key beside the style; (c) rename the built-in ids to `observatory` and `paper`. 3. **OQ-3 — The `warning` token. MAINTAINER.** Blocks AC-1.1, AC-1.7. The schema requires `warning` and three renderers use it (sha mismatch, truncation notice, diff truncation marker), but the brief gives no value and says amber means "needs you" only. Options: (a) drop the token and recolour the three uses (sha mismatch in `err` plus a word, the notices in `text_muted` plus a word); (b) keep it with the amber value, widening "needs you"; (c) keep it with a new value. 4. **OQ-4 — Does the TUI paint `surface`? MAINTAINER.** Blocks AC-1.6 and the meaning of every contrast check. Today only foreground colour is emitted (`internal/tui/diff.go:533-539`) so the terminal's own background is behind every glyph. Painting Observatory's `#090f1a` makes contrast true by construction but overrides the user's terminal background and copy / paste look (the research the design review gathered records complaints about both overreach and low-contrast stock colours). Not painting leaves contrast dependent on the terminal, so the validator proves only the theme's intended pairing.
