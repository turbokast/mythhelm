Users need evidence about accepted integrated work, defects, time, interventions and resources before choosing more complex execution policies. Operational receipts and independent acceptance must remain useful with learning disabled. Optional estimates may inform a decision, but neither observed selection patterns nor vendor capability descriptions establish that MYTHHELM is faster or better than a simpler workflow (v2 §§11–13).

The fixed portfolio has four meanings from v2 §10.2: P1 single session; P2 single writer plus review; P3 contract-bounded parallel work; P4 investigation then build. Policies share canonical task and acceptance state. Native completion, a reviewer opinion, an index hit or a high score cannot certify acceptance, grant authority or remove mandatory checks. P3's additional prerequisites must not hold baseline nonparallel outcome evidence hostage.

## Objectives

- **O1**: Inspect exact accepted-work evidence, missing measurements and later corrections independently of optional learning.
- **O2**: Choose among understandable fixed policies using scoped provenance and honest comparisons to direct-native and simpler workflows.
- **O3**: Enable, revoke, export and erase optional examples without silently authorizing backfill, exploration, telemetry or publication.
- **O4**: Produce reproducible offline reports that retain every started assignment and explain why evidence is inconclusive.

## Functional Requirements

All named checks below are prospective implementation checks, not tests claimed to exist. Each check shall retain the named negative input beside a valid positive input and prove that the violating behavior is rejected. Synthetic evidence shall remain labelled synthetic.

### FR-1 — Operational truth independent of learning (v2 §§4.1, 8.4, 11.2, 12.2)

- **AC-1.1** [I07/I09/I20/I23, AT-22/AT-28/AT-33] When an attempt produces operational evidence, the system shall bind its run/task/revision, source and dependency revisions, exact candidate and accepted integration identities, immutable acceptance contract/check definitions, verifier and environment. Only independently verified evidence may yield accepted software; native exit 0, model prose and review advice remain observations/claims. Prospective `TestOutcomeRequiresExactAcceptance` retains a successful native exit with a missing verification binding and a candidate mutated after verification.
- **AC-1.2** [I13/I22/I25, AT-33] While optional learning is disabled, empty, unavailable or corrupt, the system shall preserve admitted fixed-policy execution, protected checks, recovery and essential receipts, with an explicit optional-store failure diagnostic. Prospective `TestFixedPoliciesSurviveLearningFailure` retains a store that fails on access and an injected hidden exploration call; the latter must be detected even when normal delivery succeeds.

### FR-2 — Separate scoped consent and revocation (v2 §§5.3, 8.4, 12.1)

- **AC-2.1** [I03/I04/I19/I25, AT-27] When collection is requested, the system shall require a repository-scoped consent revision describing derived fields, purpose, retention and allowed cohort/destinations; collection, historical backfill, resource-consuming exploration, external sharing and raw capture shall be separately off by default. Collection alone shall cause zero additional native/model/network calls and shall not include raw source/task text, prompts, keystrokes, account identifiers or secrets. Prospective `TestOutcomeConsentScopes` retains collection-only consent with an attempted backfill, cross-project copy and auxiliary summarization call.
- **AC-2.2** [I06/I12/I20/I25, AT-27] When consent is revoked, the system shall fence future optional collection/updates, cancel queued experimental work and request normal stop/reconciliation for active experimental work before releasing its resources. Essential evidence remains governed by its separate operational retention contract; revocation is not a claim of erasure. Prospective `TestRevocationFencesPendingWrites` retains a queued write racing revocation and an active experiment whose stop is unconfirmed.
- **AC-2.3** [I20/I25, AT-27/AT-43] When historical backfill is requested, the system shall preview the exact eligible repository/record snapshot, intended fields and consent/normalization revisions and require separate authority before deriving examples. A changed scope/revision shall invalidate the preview. Prospective `TestBackfillRequiresExactPreview` retains old operational rows with collection consent only and a stale approved snapshot.

### FR-3 — Pre-assignment provenance (v2 §§4.1, 7.1, 9, 10.1, 12.2)

- **AC-3.1** [I02/I09/I20, AT-28] Before assignment results are available, the system shall durably freeze task features/cohort, eligible/excluded actions with reasons, selected policy/route, rationale, override source, assignment method and probability only when actually randomized. Features shall include task type, language/domain, repository maturity, scope, risk, dependencies, estimated context/capabilities and acceptance cost with source/confidence. Prospective `TestFeaturesPrecedeAssignment` retains a feature changed after the result and a deterministic choice supplied with a fabricated randomization probability.
- **AC-3.2** [I01/I04/I09/I14/I20, AT-28/AT-30] When recording a route, the system shall preserve harness/native/model alias and resolved version where known, execution surface/host/platform, effective configuration, effort, funding/entitlement evidence, trust profile and actual enforcement, qualification revision/expiry, authority, evaluator, policy, context manifest/strategy/counts and source revisions. Unknown identities shall remain unknown; a digest alone shall not establish parity, trust or entitlement. Prospective `TestRouteProvenanceCannotCertifyItself` retains identical metadata hashes with differing effective hooks/trust and an unresolved mutable model alias.

### FR-4 — Exact outcome and resource observations (v2 §§12.2, 13.1)

- **AC-4.1** [I07/I09/I23, AT-28] When an assigned attempt starts or changes evaluation disposition, the system shall retain it under `accepted`, `rejected`, `cancelled`, `timed_out`, `blocked`, `unverified` or `pending`, distinct from canonical run/task lifecycle. Every started assignment, failed/exhausted run and human rescue remains in its denominator; unfinished attempts remain pending for reconciliation. Prospective `TestAllStartedOutcomesRemain` retains one example of each label plus a worker that disappears without a finish event.
- **AC-4.2** [I09/I21, AT-28] When measurements arrive, the system shall preserve reported, observed, estimated, user-declared and unknown values distinctly, including source/version, units, coverage and normalization revision. Native cumulative counters shall be deduplicated by source/scope and never summed with their own deltas or overlapping cache counters. Missing usage/cost is not zero or a retail-price charge. Prospective `TestUsageCoverageAndMissingness` retains null categories, counter reset, duplicate cumulative frames, overlapping cache/input categories and estimates labelled as observations.
- **AC-4.3** [I09/I21, AT-28] When computing delivery time and effort, the system shall retain request-to-independent-accepted-integration time plus planning, queueing, native work, retrieval, handoff, review, integration, repair and experimental overhead where observed; unknown components remain unknown. Human interventions and maintainability/defects use a declared rubric. Cancellations/timeouts retain ceiling, censoring reason and observation window; neither becomes successful completion at its ceiling. Prospective `TestOutcomeTimingCensoring` retains a fast draft with slow verification, an interrupted clock-coverage interval and a timed-out run.

### FR-5 — Delayed corrections and validity (v2 §§9.3, 11.2, 12.2)

- **AC-5.1** [I07/I09/I20, AT-23/AT-28] When a regression, revert or later review changes an outcome's interpretation, the system shall append attributed correction evidence linked to the original, retaining original outcome, observation window, severity/rubric and unresolved attribution. It shall not overwrite history or close an unobserved defect window as clean. Prospective `TestDelayedOutcomeCorrection` retains accepted work later reverted and an unrelated defect without established attribution.
- **AC-5.2** [I07/I14/I20, AT-23/AT-28/AT-32 boundary] When source/task/dependency/candidate/check/evaluator/context-policy/environment or qualification changes materially, the system shall invalidate affected reuse/comparability, retain historical evidence with its original scope and require fresh qualification/verification as applicable. Incomplete provenance shall block comparison, not silently pool revisions. Prospective `TestOutcomeReuseInvalidation` retains a changed protected check, changed route configuration and an old report reused for a new candidate.

### FR-6 — Fixed policy portfolio (v2 §§10.1–10.3)

- **AC-6.1** [I01/I02/I05/I07/I20/I22, AT-25/AT-33] When selecting an admitted fixed workflow, the system shall preserve user pins and P1 as a candidate, implement P2 as a single writer with distinct review evidence, and P4 as bounded investigation producing a decision artifact before build when uncertainty can change the contract/route. All use immutable policy revisions, one canonical task lifecycle, unchanged mandatory checks and separately admitted native stages. Prospective `TestFixedPortfolioSemantics` retains a redundant P4 stage, a P2 reviewer self-certifying acceptance and a policy attempting to remove mandatory review.
- **AC-6.2** [I05/I18/I21/I24, AT-26, v2 §10.3] If MH-14's parallel qualification is missing or unsuitable for the task, then P3 shall be excluded with its reason while eligible P1/P2/P4 evidence proceeds. When P3 is eligible, it shall require stable contracts, isolated mutable environments, reconciled resource ownership, integration reserve and the qualified at-most-two-writer envelope. Prospective `TestP3RequiresQualifiedIsolation` retains shared hot files, a stale lease with a live writer and an unsupported third writer.

### FR-7 — Honest offline evaluation and baselines (v2 §§13.1–13.3)

- **AC-7.1** [I07/I09/I20, AT-28/AT-29/AT-30] When an offline comparison is prepared, the system shall bind task/candidate/evaluator manifests and compare direct-native use of the same qualified harness/model/configuration, a strong single-agent workflow, fixed P1/P2 and a qualified homogeneous native-child baseline where supported. A new strong model shall be considered as whole-pipeline P1; unsupported baselines remain explicitly unavailable. Source/contracts/tools/environment/budgets/human-intervention and acceptance work shall match or differences shall invalidate the affected claim. Prospective `TestBaselineParity` retains a direct-native arm stopped before review and a faster arm with wider authority or weaker tests; the unsafe arm is rejected before speed ranking.
- **AC-7.2** [I09/I20, AT-28/AT-29, v2 §13.1] When constructing a representative evaluation manifest, the system shall identify repair, feature, refactor, investigation, cross-module, stale-contract and integration tasks across small/medium repositories, a case where parallelism should lose, long sessions, quota interruptions and realistic verification. Task-family/repository units and omissions shall be explicit; synthetic fixture success shall not be described as live performance. Prospective `TestEvaluationCohortCoverage` retains a manifest containing only short successful parallel-friendly tasks.
- **AC-7.3** [I09/I20/I22, AT-29, G14 data/evaluation portion] When producing an inferential report, the system shall require a frozen pre-assignment protocol naming hypothesis, cohort, independent units, variants, budgets, quality floor, useful gain, uncertainty method, stopping rule and observation window, with separate development/holdout and recorded all-started assignments/exclusions. It shall expose paired effects/distributions, intervals, assumptions, missingness, censoring, delayed defects and multiplicity handling. Unknown parity, leakage, absent overlap, confounding, insufficient power or changed protocols shall yield an explicit invalid/inconclusive conclusion. Prospective `TestComparisonRejectsUnsupportedInference` retains harder-task selection bias, repeated task variants treated as independent, leaked holdout, post-result threshold edits and deterministic logs offered as counterfactual evidence.

### FR-8 — Recommendation-only estimates and publication boundary (v2 §§12.3–13.3)

- **AC-8.1** [I03/I20/I22/I25, AT-29/AT-33/AT-34 boundary] When optional estimates are displayed, the system shall show cohort/route/version scope, provenance, uncertainty, missingness, sample limitations and rejected alternatives. Sparse, contradictory or incomparable evidence shall retain the deterministic fixed choice. A recommendation shall not alter active policy pins, permissions, spend, protected checks or promotion rules. Prospective `TestEstimatesCannotPromote` retains a development-only win and a fabricated high-confidence score attempting to change an active pin.
- **AC-8.2** [I03/I07/I09/I22, G14] When a report is exported or used to propose an advantage claim, the system shall distinguish routine observational delivery, documented mechanism, offline fixtures, authorized live evidence, external reports and release decisions, and require separately authorized privacy-safe publication plus independent review. The existing routine ledger shall never itself establish benchmark advantage; full G14 additionally needs matched live useful-change evidence and the remaining gates. Prospective `TestReportEvidenceClasses` retains a routine-ledger speed average and a synthetic pass relabelled as measured productivity improvement.

### FR-9 — Scoped retention and erasure participation (v2 §§5.3, 8.4, 12.2)

- **AC-9.1** [I20/I23/I25, AT-27/AT-43] When examples or derived estimates are stored, the system shall link them to repository-scoped source outcomes, consent/normalization revisions and derivation roots; optional examples expire after 90 days or a shorter user policy. Operational receipts remain until explicit cleanup or configured retention, and active/pinned/dependency-referenced or ownership-ambiguous evidence shall be retained. Prospective `TestLearningRetentionRoots` retains an expired example whose operational source remains pinned and equal bytes belonging to another repository.
- **AC-9.2** [I09/I20/I25, AT-43] When scoped export/erasure is requested, the system shall consume MH-25's previewed maintenance contract and remove/invalidate/rebuild affected examples, indexes, estimates and later policies/models retaining the selected data as applicable. It shall report remaining backups/exports/native/provider copies and preserve content-free purge evidence without claiming complete erasure prematurely. Prospective `TestLearningErasurePropagation` retains a derived estimate still referencing erased content and an unhandled backup; unknown copy coverage remains unresolved.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **NFR-1** [I13/I21, v2 §§4.3, 10.3] Proposed conservative implementation caps, fixed for refinement unless the technical decision owner records a reviewed revision: one collection transaction handles at most 100 outcomes, each encoded outcome at most 64 KiB and nesting depth 64; one offline analysis handles at most 10,000 outcomes, 16 MiB aggregate encoded input and 1 MiB report with 32 MiB application buffering and a 30-second deadline. Smaller producer/native/outer limits win. At a cap, reject the operation with explicit incomplete coverage; never silently truncate an all-started denominator. Prospective `TestOutcomeBounds` retains exact-bound and one-over-bound inputs and a cancelled scan. These caps are proposed requirements, not claims about existing product limits.
- **NFR-2** [I13/I21/I25, AT-33] Optional collection/analysis shall execute zero native/model/network calls; exploration default budget is zero attempts, zero additional allowance and zero monetary authority. Foreground verification/repair reserves remain protected; later experiment budgets belong to MH-27. Credential-free deterministic fixtures must prove the offline runner using a call sentinel that fails if invoked (`TestOfflineEvaluationHasNoEffects`).
- **NFR-3** [I09/I20/I23, v2 §§4.2, 5] Canonical operational records remain the authority; the optional dataset is a reconstructible scoped projection. New public configuration/event payloads use schema_version 2; historical contracts and DB migration/plugin/release version domains remain distinct. Design shall inventory actual migrations before assigning a number and document additive compatibility/unknown-field rules, atomic consent fencing and interruption recovery. Prospective `TestOutcomeProjectionRecovery` retains a crash between source commit and projection plus a historical row with missing counters.
- **NFR-4** [I19/I25, v2 §§5.3, 8.3–8.4] Local optional evidence uses restrictive 0700 directories / 0600 regular files where supported, with profile-specific enforcement disclosure; same-user permissions shall not be advertised as hostile-worker containment. Routine redacted streaming logs follow 14-day retention, raw capture remains off, and shared/public artifacts contain only reviewed privacy-safe aggregates or labelled synthetic fixtures. Prospective `TestOutcomePrivacyBoundary` retains a raw prompt, cross-repository metadata lookup and an export without scoped preview.
