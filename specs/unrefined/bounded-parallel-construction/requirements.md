## Bounded two-writer construction and serialized integration — Requirements

> Unrefined. Run /refine-spec bounded-parallel-construction before /spec.

Normative source: `mythhelm-synthesis/MYTHHELM_Master_Spec_v2.md`, version 2.0, §§4, 6–11, 13, 17–18; implementation plan W08. The user needs two suitable parts of one requested change constructed concurrently without treating a clean merge or two native success messages as integrated correctness. P3 is useful only when task interfaces are stable, mutable environments are separate, required resources are available and completion remains affordable. Coupled work stays sequential. P1 remains available with parallel construction disabled (v2 §§10.2–10.3).

The linked issue preserves historical Stage 2 and Revision 1.1 section/gate references. The current card and adopted v2 place bounded construction in S3/W08 after scoped task/dependency foundations and applicable native-child/process qualification. The requirements below reconcile that historical vocabulary without silently changing the source issue. They consume MH-24 graph/checkpoint contracts, MH-15 context/continuity and MH-16's shipped one-agent ledger/envelopes; they do not rebuild those whole features.

### Objectives

- **O1**: Construct two independent parts concurrently with inspectable task, authority, ownership and resource boundaries.
- **O2**: Preserve both original candidates and accept only the exact integrated result after protected combined checks.
- **O3**: Stop, exhaust allowance, lose an owner or encounter a conflict without duplicate attempts, unsafe capacity reuse or lost work.
- **O4**: Keep a useful P1 workflow and honest capability/resource receipts when P3 is disabled or unqualified.

## Functional Requirements

All check names below are prospective acceptance checks, not existing test/API claims. Each implementation check retains the stated broken input and a positive case reaching the same decision/effect seam; refusal-only or empty-observation success is insufficient.

### FR-1 — Explicit optional P3 eligibility and frozen contracts

- **AC-1.1** [I02/I20/I23 (v2 §2), v2 §§9.3, 10.2–10.3] When P3 is requested, the system shall freeze the plan/policy revision and each of the two task revisions with deliverable, exact acceptance contract, dependency/checkpoint revisions and kinds, read inputs/scopes, write scope, resource claims, integration destination/order and check responsibility before dispatch. `TestP3FrozenContracts` shall retain missing read-input, mutable checkpoint and unknown critical contract-field cases that fail validation before any launch intent.
- **AC-1.2** [I03/I04/I17 (v2 §2), G07 (v2 §18.3), v2 §§8.2, 10.1] If task text, pane state, model output or a contract proposes broader permissions, native calls, account changes or external effects, then the system shall treat it as untrusted data and require existing applicable authority plus admission. `TestP3ContractCannotGrant` shall retain injected launch/spend/accept directives and assert the admitted grant set and observed launcher/effect calls, including an explicitly authorized positive control.
- **AC-1.3** [I05/I21/I25 (v2 §2), v2 §§10.2–10.3] When tasks share hot write paths, unstable required interfaces, inseparable external state or inadequate completion reserve, the system shall explain the failed separability condition and retain a qualified P1/sequential option within existing authority. With P3 disabled, the ordinary P1 candidate/check/review workflow shall remain usable. `TestP3EligibilityAndDisabledP1` shall retain a coupled pair that would textually merge but must not overlap, and an independent pair that is actually eligible.

### FR-2 — Atomic shared resource and funding admission

- **AC-2.1** [I02/I05/I18/I24 (v2 §2), AT-19/AT-20 (v2 §18.2)] When scheduling an eligible pair, the system shall acquire each complete required process/workspace/file/external-resource/billing claim set atomically in stable order through the canonical ledger, respecting host-wide active reservations and the two-writer ceiling. `TestP3AtomicClaims` shall race reversed claim orders, a third writer and a shared port/database claim; failed acquisitions shall leave no partial held set, and disjoint acquisitions shall produce two real owned reservations.
- **AC-2.2** [I09/I10/I15/I19 (v2 §2), G05 (v2 §18.3), v2 §7.3] When reserving parent, child, verification or repair work, the system shall bind their entire declared execution/funding tree to qualified included-only routes and coupled allowance buckets, retaining source/unit/scope/time and reported/observed/estimated/user-declared/unknown labels. Additional names, accounts or subscriptions shall neither create virtual capacity nor bypass the two-writer ceiling; local reservations shall not claim provider quota guarantees. `TestP3CoupledAllowance` shall retain duplicate-bucket profiles, absent usage, purchased-credit continuation and an included-only unknown-remaining route with qualified stop-at-exhaustion.
- **AC-2.3** [I02/I10/I21 (v2 §2), AT-26 (v2 §18.2), v2 §10.3] When optional parallel work is considered, the system shall first preserve one combined verification pass and the admitted repair envelope, prioritize foreground critical-path work and use bounded ageing for queued eligible work. If capacity is unavailable, then it shall queue or block without silently consuming completion reserve. `TestP3CompletionReserveAndFairness` shall retain an exhausted reserve, repeated competing arrivals and a preemption whose previous owner has not stopped; only reconciled capacity may be reused.

### FR-3 — Separate owned mutable environments and qualified native children

- **AC-3.1** [I05/I18/I24 (v2 §2), AT-20 (v2 §18.2), v2 §10.3] When either writer starts, the system shall bind one process owner and one native input writer to its attempt, distinct source snapshot/workspace and separately owned build output, temporary directories, mutable caches, ports, test databases and external resources. Shared immutable inputs may be read within the contract; declared shared mutations shall serialize. `TestP3MutableEnvironmentIsolation` shall retain deliberately shared cache/database/port and aliasing-path fixtures, and verify each writer's actual effects rather than directory names alone.
- **AC-3.2** [I01/I02/I14/I15/I18 (v2 §2), AT-19 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§7.1, 10.3] If native child count, mutable-workspace behavior, included funding or stop propagation cannot be bounded for the exact execution bundle, then the system shall disable the mode with a visible fidelity disclosure or block a task that requires it. All simultaneously mutating native children shall consume the same two-writer/resource envelope; a cooperative prompt shall not qualify enforcement. `TestP3ChildEnvelopeAdmission` shall retain an undeclared child, unbounded spawn, missing child billing and escaped-child stop cases; live-enabled claims additionally require versioned native evidence.

### FR-4 — Revision fences and mandatory context

- **AC-4.1** [I07/I20/I23 (v2 §2), AT-18 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§6.2, 9.3] When a required task/dependency/interface checkpoint changes, the system shall consume MH-24 invalidation, fence new dispatch and acceptance, and recheck revisions/generation at candidate ingestion and integration. Stale artifacts shall remain inspectable without becoming accepted; reuse shall require explicit justified revalidation or a new revision. `TestP3StaleWriterResult` shall retain a changed contract while both writers run and a late old-generation success result.
- **AC-4.2** [I03/I20/I23 (v2 §2), G13 (v2 §18.3), v2 §§9.1–9.3] When preparing either writer or a repair, the system shall supply MH-15/MH-24's exact mandatory originals, accepted decisions, dependency revisions, scope/authority/stop limits and unresolved evidence in a scoped immutable context manifest. Interface-checkpoint acceptance shall not certify producer implementation. `TestP3WriterContext` shall retain an omitted original-only constraint, wrong-repository dependency and changed checkpoint; mandatory-core overflow shall block or require grounded decomposition, never silent truncation.

### FR-5 — Freeze and serialize exact combined integration

- **AC-5.1** [I05/I07/I24 (v2 §2), G06 (v2 §18.3), v2 §§6.3, 11.1] When a writer yields a candidate, the system shall freeze its content-addressed tree/commit and artifact manifest only after proven quiescence, preserving its base and task/context/resource lineage. `TestP3FreezeAfterQuiescence` shall retain zero native exit with an active descendant, a late file write and a settled positive case; native success alone shall not permit freezing while a writer remains active.
- **AC-5.2** [I07/I08/I20 (v2 §2), AT-21/AT-23 (v2 §18.2)] When candidates enter integration, the system shall serialize composition in a disposable environment against the latest accepted integration base, rechecking expected refs, contracts, dependencies and cancellation fences before each advance. `TestP3SerializedLatestBase` shall retain two simultaneous queue consumers, an advancing base and duplicate composition operation; only one authorized integration result may advance, with the original checkout files/index/refs unchanged.
- **AC-5.3** [I07/I20/I22 (v2 §2), G06 (v2 §18.3), v2 §§11.1–11.2] When the composed source is checked, the system shall use protected combined acceptance independent of candidate/native assertions and bind Verification to exact final source/base/dependencies, contract, immutable checks, evaluator/toolchain/environment and required review evidence. `TestP3SemanticConflict` shall retain a pair whose component checks pass and clean textual merge fails a real combined behavior check; `TestP3ProtectedCombinedChecks` shall retain removed/weakened checks and changed environment/base/cache-key inputs. None shall become accepted through component success or stale cache reuse.

### FR-6 — Bounded repair, exhaustion and truthful acceptance

- **AC-6.1** [I07/I20/I21 (v2 §2), AT-21 (v2 §18.2), v2 §11.1] If textual or semantic integration fails and the shared repair envelope permits work, then the system shall create a new admitted repair attempt with current base, both preserved candidates, conflict evidence and original requirements; it shall never overwrite either producer input or repair concurrently in an active producer workspace. `TestP3RepairPreservesCandidates` shall retain conflicting content and a zero-repair envelope, compare original candidate digests and observe shared counter consumption.
- **AC-6.2** [I02/I09/I15/I21 (v2 §2), G05 (v2 §18.3), v2 §§7.3, 10.3] If allowance, execution, repair, replan or required resource capacity is exhausted, then the system shall preserve candidates/session evidence, stop admitting affected native work and expose a specific blocked reason. It shall offer only known-reset waiting or an already authorized, newly admitted qualified alternative; unknown reset stays unknown. `TestP3SharedEnvelopeExhaustion` shall retain exhaustion during both drafting and repair, an external consumer's quota exhaustion and a proposed paid summary/fallback, asserting no new affected launch and no budget reset.
- **AC-6.3** [I07/I09/I20 (v2 §2), AT-22–AT-24 (v2 §18.2)] When checks are unavailable, skipped or invalid, the system shall preserve a visibly unverified candidate, with unavailable required checks yielding exit 5 and no accepted-software/verified-ready claim. Accepted tasks shall reference the actual integrated revision; later changes require fresh affected verification without rewriting history. `TestP3CombinedVerificationGaps` shall retain a missing tool, skipped required suite, self-certified result and later changed integration tree; a deliberately limited draft contract shall remain distinct from verified software.

### FR-7 — Stop the complete tree and reconcile uncertainty

- **AC-7.1** [I06/I12/I18/I24 (v2 §2), G04 (v2 §18.3), v2 §§6.1–6.4] When pause, stop, supersession or revocation occurs during drafting, queueing, integration or verification, the system shall fence new dispatch/effects/acceptance immediately as applicable, stop scheduling descendants and use each qualified finite stop ladder for all owned mutation. Pause alone shall allow admitted turns to continue while charging time until quiescence. `TestP3CancelEveryPhase` shall retain stop/completion races and approval-wait/detached-child cases, observing both writer identities and integration/check processes; cancellation shall become final only after owned local mutation stops and unresolved effects are accounted for.
- **AC-7.2** [I06/I12/I24 (v2 §2), AT-08/AT-09/AT-20 (v2 §18.2)] If a stop deadline expires, a lease expires, an owner disappears or an external-effect acknowledgement is ambiguous, then the system shall retain/quarantine ownership with explicit uncertainty and a reconciliation owner/next action. `TestP3UncertainOwnerRecovery` shall retain PID reuse, escaped child, lost effect acknowledgement and supervisor restart; one bounded reconciliation pass may reconnect only the same proven live episode or preserve/block artifacts, never automatically relaunch, replay tools or reclaim capacity on timeout alone.
- **AC-7.3** [I03/I08/I12/I17 (v2 §2), v2 §§6.3–6.4, 11.3] When a fallback or explicit apply is requested after parallel failure, the system shall first reconcile old owners/effects, then validate the exact new attempt/effect authority and candidate/expected destination state. `TestP3NoConcurrentFallbackOrDoubleApply` shall retain restored panes, duplicate requests, response loss after successful apply and a competing ref update; it shall observe at most the authorized effect and preserve unrelated source work. Publication/deployment remain separately authorized effects.

### FR-8 — Inspectable capability and outcome receipts

- **AC-8.1** [I09/I14/I25 (v2 §2), v2 §§13.1, 17.2] When reporting a P3 decision or result, the system shall expose policy/task revisions, why two writers were eligible or refused, native bundle/fidelity, owner/reservation/queue status, candidate/integration/verification identities, shared deadlines/counters and unknown usage/effects with provenance. `TestP3ReceiptTruth` shall retain native-success-but-unverified, quarantined child, missing counters and successful combined acceptance; fixtures shall distinguish all outcomes without secret/transcript disclosure.
- **AC-8.2** [I01/I13/I14/I25 (v2 §2), G04–G07 (v2 §18.3), v2 §§7.1, 18.1–18.3] If only offline fixtures or contract work pass, then the system/documentation shall label live parallel capability unqualified/disabled and preserve native/P1 use. `TestP3QualificationClaims` shall retain fixture-only, expired and wrong-version child/stop evidence beside a valid scoped bundle. Faster-delivery or broader native/platform claims shall require their own authorized matched evidence, not a writer count or elapsed fixture timing.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **NFR-1** [I21 (v2 §2), v2 §10.3] The initial P3 profile shall cap total simultaneous construction writers at 2 across parent/native-child mutation, shared execution at 30 minutes, total repair attempts at 2, material replans at 2 and transient transport retries at 3 per operation. Execution starts with first execution including admitted planning; native waiting/throttling count, only quiescent user pause is excluded. Smaller active grant, configuration, resource or qualified native limits win. A larger profile requires a recorded authorized extension and applicable qualification; neither reconnect nor policy switching resets consumed budgets. `TestP3FiniteLimits` retains each cap+1 and deadline/pause boundary.
- **NFR-2** [I21 (v2 §2), v2 §§6.4, 10.3] Stop/reconciliation shall use a separately declared deadline no greater than 60 seconds for the initial supported P3 profile, or the smaller qualified boundary. At expiry, report incomplete cancellation/quarantine and stop signaling ambiguous owners. One unresolved reconciliation pass shall require fresh evidence or user action before another pass. `TestP3StopDeadline` uses a controllable clock and retains a nonterminating child; the exact supported ladder remains a qualification prerequisite, not an assumed implementation fact.
- **NFR-3** [I20/I21/I23 (v2 §2), v2 §§4, 10.3] Initial offline/live P3 contract validation shall accept at most 16 plan tasks, 64 dependency edges and 32 resource claims per task, with the two construction writers plus bounded repair/integration governed above. It shall reject oversized/cyclic contracts before allocation/dispatch. Scheduling one such ready-set/reservation decision shall finish within 1 second on the declared contributor reference environment, excluding native/check runtime; `TestP3BoundedPlan` retains exact-limit, limit+1 and cycle fixtures. Mandatory context uses the smaller producer/native bounds and shall never be truncated to meet these limits.
- **NFR-4** [I09/I13/I14 (v2 §2), v2 §18.2] Every prospective check shall run with synthetic deterministic fixtures and no native credentials, network service, paid invocation or experiments. Live child/stop/funding and meaningful performance qualification remain separately authorized evidence scoped to exact versions/platforms. `TestP3OfflineSuite` retains a network/native-launch trap and a deliberately invalid accepted-result record; routine receipt collection shall not enable optional learning/telemetry.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] FR-1–FR-8 and NFR-1–NFR-4 have named positive and retained broken-input checks that reach their actual decision/effect seam; absence assertions have a demonstrated red counterfactual.
- [ ] Independent acceptance observes two distinct active owned construction episodes for the independent pair, a coupled pair remaining sequential, and individually passing candidates failing a combined semantic check; a worker count alone is insufficient.
- [ ] Candidate preservation, exact final-source verification, stale/cancel fences, shared ceilings, complete-child stop and uncertain-owner/effect recovery are verified without granting authority through prose or restoring panes.
- [ ] P1 remains useful with P3 disabled; receipts distinguish fixture-tested from live-qualified. G04/G05/G06 and applicable G02/G03/G07/G13/G16 requirements are traced to scoped evidence before advertised activation; this document closes none of those gates.
- [ ] Fresh refinement and later design reconcile actual producer schemas/APIs, ADR needs and qualification evidence; domain-pure implementation tasks follow reviewed producer-before-consumer contracts. Required repository gates/review and maintainer checkpoints remain in force.
