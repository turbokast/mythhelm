## Context

- **Backlog card**: MH-6
- **Source issue**: [#27 — Plugin protocol and declarative themes](https://github.com/turbokast/mythhelm/issues/27), historical Revision 1.1 scope; the adopted card's v2 §§8/16 and W09 govern this capture.
- **Grounding revision**: `efcbf90` on `origin/main`. Re-ground before refinement/design. Adjacent `rg` commands locate definitions when line numbers move.

Users should extend a useful local workflow without forking the core or trusting plugin output as permission. A process plugin can propose a route, invocation or finding; only the admitted owner can launch native work, authorize effects, record acceptance or reconcile an uncertain outcome. A declarative workflow is reviewable instruction-bearing data, not an alternate execution engine.

The existing private seams and themes are starting points. Stabilization requires two real built-in consumers for each interface being advertised, then a bounded external contract and an offline contributor journey. An example or negotiated protocol does not prove that a real native harness, platform, entitlement or security boundary is qualified.

## Objectives

- **O1**: Install, inspect, separately enable, test, disable and revoke a useful local extension without paid credentials or a required service.
- **O2**: Publish one small negotiated contract exercised by real built-ins and accessible to an external Go contributor.
- **O3**: Keep admission, immutable evidence, native identity, process/input ownership and effect approval authoritative under malicious or failing plugin input.
- **O4**: Review declarative workflow changes and restore safe built-in controls even when a mod is invalid.

## Functional Requirements

Every named check below is prospective. Implementation retains a successful case and each named violating case; no check is claimed to exist or pass from this document alone.

### FR-1 — Stabilize a bounded, exercised public subset

- **AC-1.1** [I11/I14 (v2 §2), G08 (v2 §18.3), v2 §16] When preparing to freeze any public interface, the system shall retain an inspectable consumer-to-interface matrix showing at least two distinct real built-in implementations exercising each advertised contract through production composition, including error behavior. `BuiltinContractExercise` shall reject a matrix backed only by two wrappers over one fixture or two test-only mocks; the wire/schema design remains a later design deliverable.
- **AC-1.2** [I11/I13 (v2 §2), v2 §16] When publishing the initial subset, the system shall support deterministic eligible-route ranking, scripted adapter proposals/observations, advisory-versus-authorized verifier evidence, and declarative theme/layout/keymap/workflow/routing/prompt data. `PublicSubsetMatrix` shall enumerate supported operations and unsupported future families, rejecting an undeclared context/export/host operation rather than invoking a generic shell/action fallback. This defines capability families, not provider selection.

### FR-2 — Independent versions and explicit negotiation

- **AC-2.1** [I02/I09/I11/I14 (v2 §2), AT-38 (v2 §18.2), v2 §16] When a process plugin starts, the broker shall complete a mandatory identity/version handshake for `mythhelm-plugin` major 1 over dedicated stdin/stdout JSON-RPC 2.0 before ordinary requests. Manifest version 1, protocol major 1, canonical event/config schema 2 and the existing adapter capability-record version shall remain distinct version domains. `PluginHandshakeCompatibility` shall retain incompatible major, missing handshake and cross-domain-version substitution cases, each refused before useful work.
- **AC-2.2** [I02/I09/I11 (v2 §2), AT-38 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§7/16] When negotiating optional features and broker capabilities, the broker shall record requested, implemented, qualified where required, granted and effective sets separately. Only their applicable intersection may be used; unknown required capability/evidence, an unknown authority-bearing field, an unnegotiated method or a denied request shall fail closed. `PluginCapabilityIntersection` shall retain a plugin that claims supported while host qualification is unknown and one that requests an ungranted effect; neither may gain authority.
- **AC-2.3** [I14/I20 (v2 §2), AT-38 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§5/16] When a compatible optional feature is added or an incompatible change proposed, the published compatibility policy shall demonstrate old-peer/new-peer behavior against version-pinned golden fixtures, require explicit negotiation for optional behavior, and require a new major for incompatible semantics. `PluginEvolutionMatrix` shall reject an older peer silently accepting a new mandatory authority key; active attempts retain their pinned contract/binary rather than upgrading in place.

### FR-3 — Bounded transport and validated data

- **AC-3.1** [I11/I21 (v2 §2), AT-38 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§4.5/16] When exchanging frames, the broker shall enforce unique outstanding request IDs, explicit deadlines, typed canonical error mapping and NFR-1/2 bounds before dispatch or allocation beyond those bounds. `PluginTransportConformance` shall retain duplicate IDs, invalid UTF-8/JSON, truncated/oversized/deep frames, trailing data, response-ID mismatch and output-flood cases; each must reach the parser and produce a bounded refusal/disable outcome instead of an ignored fixture.
- **AC-3.2** [I03/I11/I20 (v2 §2), AT-38 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§4/8.3/16] When accepting plugin data or proposals, the broker shall validate typed schema, immutable task/artifact/source revisions, scope and finite resource claims before admitting the proposal. `PluginProposalValidation` shall reject cycles, stale revisions, foreign-repository references, path/argv substitution, shell-interpolated commands, nonfinite values and fabricated acceptance. Absent measurements remain unknown; a valid syntax-only proposal cannot mark work accepted.

### FR-4 — Core-owned effects, native execution and trust

- **AC-4.1** [I01/I02/I03/I04/I11/I16/I18/I19 (v2 §2), AT-12/AT-47 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§7/8/16] When an adapter plugin proposes execution, the core shall revalidate the exact native executable identity, argv, environment policy, working directory, selected harness/surface, billing and applicable grants before its worker launches once. `PluginNativeOwnership` shall retain substituted SDK/model routes, raw native-subscription secret requests, credential-bearing argv, direct-launch requests and a second native input writer; all shall refuse before the prohibited effect. Native sign-in stays native, and public Go SDK code implements the process protocol rather than replacing the selected harness with an inference SDK.
- **AC-4.2** [I03/I07/I11/I20/I22/I23 (v2 §2), AT-12/AT-38 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§8/11/16] If plugin text, recipe content or evidence asks to approve, publish, widen grants, change protected checks or mutate canonical state directly, then only a separately authorized core operation may act, bound to exact revision/artifact/destination and expected state. `PluginAuthorityIsolation` shall retain forged approval, self-selected trusted verifier, forged completion and attachment-token-as-execution-grant cases; effect counters and exact canonical acceptance records shall demonstrate refusal, with a deliberately bypassing implementation variant making the check fail.
- **AC-4.3** [I03/I10/I11/I14 (v2 §2), AT-47 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§8.1/16] When enabling native/interpreter extension code, the user shall separately approve its disclosed trusted-host access or use a qualified enforced boundary covering its startup, descendants and relevant resources. `PluginTrustProfileCoverage` shall refuse missing approval, unsupported containment and a plugin that defeats the requested evaluator/controller isolation. Process separation and broker allowlists shall never be labeled containment against arbitrary same-user code; no supported plugin API shall load arbitrary untrusted Go code in the controller process.

### FR-5 — Cancellation, failure isolation and exact recovery

- **AC-5.1** [I06/I11/I18/I21 (v2 §2), AT-38 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§6/16] When a request expires, is cancelled, loses health or its plugin crashes/hangs/floods, the broker shall stop accepting affected work, use its bounded shutdown/stop path and preserve canonical evidence. `PluginLifecycleFaults` shall retain an ignored cancellation, blocked stderr and crash-after-proposal case; a cancellation acknowledgement shall not certify stopped native descendants or release unresolved writer ownership.
- **AC-5.2** [I02/I12/I18/I20/I24 (v2 §2), AT-38 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§6.4/16] When reconnecting or recovering, the system shall reconcile exact plugin artifact/version/instance, controller generation, run/task revision, attempt/launch/native-session identities and prior operations before reuse. `PluginReconnectIdentity` shall refuse swapped binary, stale generation, unknown launch and duplicate response/effect replay; observation replay shall never execute tools or resubmit prompts. Uncertain effects remain unresolved until reconciled, with no automatic relaunch or lease-based proof of termination.
- **AC-5.3** [I02/I07/I09/I11 (v2 §2), AT-38 (v2 §18.2), v2 §16] If an optional router/context operation fails, then the core shall use its documented deterministic/exact-reference fallback within the admitted route and record degraded provenance; if a required adapter/verifier fails, then only dependent work shall block. `PluginFailurePolicy` shall retain a required-verifier failure and pinned-harness failure that an unsafe implementation silently replaces; the check shall observe the exact selected route and acceptance state, not just a warning.

### FR-6 — Inspectable installation, pins, revocation and uninstall

- **AC-6.1** [I03/I11/I14 (v2 §2), AT-38/AT-47 (v2 §18.2), v2 §16] When inspecting a local bundle or explicit repository artifact, the system shall show identity/version/license, architecture, executable digests, runtime dependencies, execution trust, requested capabilities and configuration schema, then verify artifact/source provenance before installation and require a separate enable decision. `PluginInstallJourney` shall retain missing identity, unsupported architecture, undeclared executable, self-asserted-signature-only and install-as-enable cases; listed, identity-verified, conformance-tested and security-reviewed shall remain distinct statuses.
- **AC-6.2** [I03/I11/I21 (v2 §2), AT-47 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§8.3/16] When unpacking a bundle, the installer shall enforce NFR-3 and refuse traversal, absolute paths, symlink/junction escape, case-colliding paths, unexpected output roots, extraction bombs and install scripts before activation. `PluginArchiveBoundary` shall retain each violating archive and a valid bounded archive; a race-sensitive guarantee requires an established enforcement mechanism, not only a string-prefix path check.
- **AC-6.3** [I04/I11/I20 (v2 §2), AT-38 (v2 §18.2), v2 §16] When enabling or updating, the installation lock shall bind verified artifact/source identity, exact version/digest, configuration revision and actual granted capabilities; permission/license/trust changes shall require review before new use. `PluginPinUpdate` shall retain an on-disk executable swap, added capability and same-name/different-source update; active attempts keep the original pin and cannot silently adopt the update.
- **AC-6.4** [I06/I11/I12/I20 (v2 §2), AT-38 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§6/16] When a capability is revoked or an extension disabled, new affected requests shall refuse immediately and active affected work shall enter the bounded stop/reconciliation path without deleting evidence. `PluginRevocationRace` shall retain revoke-during-dispatch and revoke-after-effect-intent cases; neither may authorize another effect, while already uncertain external effects remain visible rather than being retried or declared undone.

- **AC-6.5** [I03/I06/I11/I12/I20/I25 (v2 §2), AT-38/AT-47 (v2 §18.2), G08 (v2 §18.3), v2 §§6/8.4/16, implementation plan W09] When a separately authorised uninstall names an installed extension, the system shall remove it from future installed availability and, after active ownership is conclusively reconciled, remove only its manifest-owned installation bytes and registrations within NFR-3 limits. Active or uncertain references shall defer destructive cleanup with an inspectable bounded disposition; protected evidence, canonical history and immutable active-attempt pins shall remain intact. Repeated uninstall shall be idempotent and leave built-in baseline behavior usable. `PluginUninstallJourney` shall retain an active pinned attempt, uncertain child, foreign or escaping target, repeated request and partial-removal failure; its quiescent positive control must observe both absent installed availability and removed owned bytes while evidence and unrelated installations remain unchanged.

### FR-7 — Declarative workflows preserve critical controls

- **AC-7.1** [I03/I11/I20/I21/I23 (v2 §2), AT-39 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§9.3/16] When importing a declarative workflow, routing rule or prompt pack, the system shall validate explicit pinned acyclic dependencies and treat instruction-bearing content as a reviewable proposal whose tasks/effects use canonical contracts, grants, resource envelopes and protected checks. `DeclarativeWorkflowSafety` shall retain imports/install scripts/shell evaluation, cyclic dependencies, hidden acceptance edits, unpinned references and parallel requests before MH-14 qualification; rejection shall leave the original canonical revision and controls usable.
- **AC-7.2** [I03/I11/I13/I25 (v2 §2), AT-39 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§15/16] When applying theme/keymap/layout data, the system shall preserve visible trusted warnings, approval scope, stop/pause and required check status, and provide a documented reset command independent of damaged keybindings. `ModCriticalControlReset` shall retain a layout hiding approval scope, keymap suppressing stop/reset navigation, executable theme key and unreadable contrast case; valid changes remain usable in accessible/plain modes without custom fonts.

### FR-8 — Sanitized provenance and scoped data

- **AC-8.1** [I03/I09/I11/I19/I20/I23 (v2 §2), AT-12/AT-47 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§8.3/9/16] When recording or displaying plugin provenance, the system shall retain bounded typed source/artifact identity, protocol/grant/config revisions and effect/evidence links, distinguish plugin claims from verified facts, and sanitize hostile terminal controls, hyperlinks, bidi and filenames. `PluginProvenanceSafety` shall retain control/bidi payloads, forged core-origin labels and synthetic secret sentinels; logs/errors/receipts shall disclose neither secrets nor foreign scoped content. Raw native transcripts/subscription secrets shall not be imported as provenance.
- **AC-8.2** [I03/I09/I13/I25 (v2 §2), AT-47 (v2 §18.2), v2 §§8.3–8.4/16] When a plugin requests content or network access, the broker shall authorize exact repository/run/source and destination scope before retrieval or egress; optional catalogue/telemetry refresh remains off by default. `PluginScopeAndOffline` shall retain foreign-existing/foreign-missing probes and an undeclared endpoint; the offline journey must still complete, and broker denial shall not be described as preventing ambient egress from an approved unrestricted trusted-host executable.

### FR-9 — Public SDK, documentation and credential-free conformance

- **AC-9.1** [I11/I13/I14 (v2 §2), G08 (v2 §18.3), v2 §16] When publishing the public subset, the system shall provide language-neutral JSON Schemas, versioned protocol/error/compatibility documentation, golden fixtures, a conformance runner, Go reference SDK and credential-free theme/layout, deterministic router, scripted native adapter and advisory-versus-trusted verifier examples. `PublicContributorJourney` shall build and run the examples through the actual install/enable/broker/owner/reset/uninstall/revoke path from published instructions; a fixture-only or mock-only journey shall fail, and unsupported future families shall be labeled explicitly.
- **AC-9.2** [I07/I09/I13/I14 (v2 §2), AT-38/AT-39/AT-47 (v2 §18.2), G08/G12 (v2 §18.3), v2 §16] When `plugins test` evaluates an extension, it shall produce an inspectable version/digest/capability-scoped result covering negotiation, cancellation, malformed/overload inputs, invalid proposals, trust/revocation and the advertised mod classes. `PluginConformanceReport` shall retain a deliberately nonconforming plugin and missing-evidence row, returning failure/unknown rather than passed; conformance shall not imply native fidelity, entitlement, universal safety or alternative-surface G12 qualification.

## Non-Functional Requirements

These are proposed bounded defaults for fresh refinement, not claims of implemented limits. A lower admitted resource envelope always wins; a higher configured value needs a reviewed finite ceiling and fresh applicable authority.

- **NFR-1** [I11/I21 (v2 §2), v2 §§4/16]: Encoded plugin frames shall be at most 1 MiB, nesting depth 64 and artifact references 128 per frame; each plugin instance shall have at most 16 outstanding requests and 32 queued frames. Retained stderr diagnostics shall be at most 64 KiB per instance with a 16 KiB/s intake ceiling. `PluginTransportBounds` retains exact-limit and limit-plus-one cases and a stalled reader/flood fixture; outcome/evidence records shall never be silently dropped to keep a queue green.
- **NFR-2** [I06/I10/I11/I21 (v2 §2), v2 §§6/16]: Handshake and shutdown shall each have a 5-second budget; ordinary requests default to 30 seconds and never exceed the remaining admitted envelope. Plugin helper lifetime is at most the lesser of the admitted attempt lifetime and 1 hour; automatic restart budget defaults to zero. A 10-second stalled-plugin fixture shall leave independent authorized status and control acknowledgements responsive within 2 seconds. `PluginDeadlineIsolation` retains a deliberately blocking broker variant which violates that bound. These are broker/time limits, not an unqualified OS CPU/memory/spending cap; required hard resource isolation blocks unless enforced coverage exists.
- **NFR-3** [I11/I21 (v2 §2), v2 §§8.3/16]: Initial bundles shall be bounded to 32 MiB compressed, 128 MiB total expanded, 1,024 entries and a 100:1 expansion ratio. `PluginArchiveLimits` retains exact-boundary/plus-one fixtures for every dimension and aborts without activation or writes outside staging. Dependency traversal shall stop at 128 nodes/depth 32; larger graphs refuse explicitly.
- **NFR-4** [I13/I14/I25 (v2 §2), v2 §§16–18]: Normal examples, conformance and contributor checks shall require zero native credentials, inference, optional service, external telemetry or paid auxiliaries. `PublicOfflineJourney` shall run with network unavailable and fake credentials absent. Any additional live/human/platform suite remains separately authorized and reports exact tested versions plus unsupported/unknown rows.
