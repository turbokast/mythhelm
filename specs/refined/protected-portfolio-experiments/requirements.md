Optional experiments should resolve a specific user decision without consuming foreground completion capacity or changing correctness, security or spending rules. The first useful result may be that P1 wins, the available budget cannot distinguish a useful difference, or the comparison is invalid. A development-only gain, repeated favorable peeking or routine delivery average cannot establish product advantage (v2 §§12–13; W11).

The fixed portfolio is P1 one native session/one writer/protected checks; P2 one writer plus independent review; P3 contract-bounded parallel work; P4 investigation then build (v2 §10.2). Compare only existing eligible qualified versions, including whole pipelines and simpler single-agent alternatives. Parallel variants need MH-14/W08; sequential preparation and nonparallel comparisons do not wait for the entire parallel portfolio. Native completion and optimization scores remain separate from independent acceptance.

## Objectives

- **O1**: Freeze a specific hypothesis, comparable qualified variants, protected evaluator and finite allowance before any assignment.
- **O2**: Preserve foreground delivery and account for every experimental start, auxiliary call, missing outcome and delayed defect.
- **O3**: Produce an inspectable recommendation or invalid/inconclusive/rejected result without changing policy pins or authority.
- **O4**: Begin with a small bounded credential-free offline cohort while keeping live evidence gates explicit.

## Functional Requirements

Each named check below is prospective, not claimed to exist. Every check must retain the specified violating input beside a valid positive input and independently observe rejection or the required state/effect. Fixture outputs remain labelled synthetic.

### FR-1 — Immutable registration before assignment (v2 §§4.1, 12.2, 13.2)

- **AC-1.1** [I09/I20/I21, AT-28/AT-29, G14] When registering an experiment, the system shall freeze its identity/revision, hypothesis and decision, repository/task cohort, independent sampling unit, inclusion/exclusion rules, qualified variant/policy revisions, development/holdout split, assignment method, metrics and non-safety margins, useful gain, uncertainty/power method, budgets, stopping rule and observation window before assignment. Missing mandatory fields or nonfinite/nonpositive enabled budgets shall block launch. Prospective `TestExperimentRegistrationComplete` retains a missing observation window, NaN allowance and a protocol first registered after results.
- **AC-1.2** [I20/I22/I23, AT-28/AT-30] If the cohort, variant, evaluator, budget or decision rule materially changes, then the system shall create a new immutable revision and invalidate affected authority/comparability rather than overwrite registered inputs. Canonical `Experiment` and `PolicyVersion` remain the contract identities; annotations and evaluation dispositions shall not create alternative run states. Prospective `TestExperimentRevisionCannotRewriteHistory` retains an in-place favorable threshold edit and a policy revision whose content digest changes.

### FR-2 — Scoped opt-in and qualified effects (v2 §§7–8, 12.3–12.4)

- **AC-2.1** [I02–I04/I15/I16/I21/I25, AT-03/AT-04/AT-27, G05] While exploration is disabled or lacks separate repository/cohort-scoped experiment authority, the system shall perform zero optional experiment/native/model calls and allocate zero exploration attempts/allowance; collection/backfill/telemetry consent shall not authorize exploration. Enabling exploration shall require finite run/time/resource bounds and exact eligible routes for every implementer, proposer, reviewer, summary, child and evaluator call. Prospective `TestExplorationRequiresSeparateAuthority` retains collection-only consent and a hidden summarizer with no route grant.
- **AC-2.2** [I01/I02/I04/I09/I14–I16/I19, AT-03/AT-04/AT-30/AT-32 boundary, G05] When admitting each experimental effect, the system shall recheck actual harness/model/configuration/host/trust versions, capability and entitlement/no-paid-continuation evidence, expiry/drift, authority and required boundaries; unknown mandatory evidence shall block that effect. Exhaustion or drift shall preserve work and block further affected calls without paid/API fallback, native installation, credential extraction or wider authority. Prospective `TestExperimentQualificationCannotBorrowParent` retains expired entitlement, a changed startup hook and a paid auxiliary hidden under an included parent.

### FR-3 — Matched variants and sealed acceptance (v2 §§10.2, 11.2, 13.1–13.2)

- **AC-3.1** [I01/I07/I09/I14/I20/I22, AT-22/AT-23/AT-30, G14] When comparing a candidate route/context/prompt or whole pipeline, the system shall bind matched source snapshots, task/dependency contracts, required context, tools, environment, native settings/effort, trust/authority, resource and human-intervention rules, plus independent acceptance costs. Each bounded two-arm protocol shall pair a challenger with one preregistered control; the comparison program shall cover same-qualified direct-native use and appropriate strong simpler fixed P1/P2 workflows in separately budgeted protocols. Missing relevant controls shall limit conclusions explicitly; homogeneous native-child and P3 controls require their own qualification. Unknown/mismatched parity shall prevent a comparative advantage conclusion. Prospective `TestExperimentMatchedControls` retains a baseline stopped before review, differing source/context and an unqualified child control.
- **AC-3.2** [I03/I07/I20/I22, AT-12/AT-22/AT-23/AT-30, G14] When evaluating any variant, the system shall use a frozen evaluator/check definitions, candidate/contract/environment binding and independently protected correctness/security/authority gate inaccessible to candidate agents and optimization metrics. A score, native exit, advisory reviewer or faster result shall never override it; legitimate evaluator changes require a separately reviewed contract revision and fresh verification. Prospective `TestExperimentSealedAcceptance` retains a worker-modified test, replaced grader prompt, hidden exit status and fast candidate with wider authority.

### FR-4 — Assignment, leakage and all-started accounting (v2 §§12.2, 13.2)

- **AC-4.1** [I09/I20/I23, AT-28/AT-29, G14] Before dispatch, the system shall persist pre-result features, eligible/excluded variants with reasons, assignment method/order, actual probabilities only for randomized assignment, cohort/split and protocol/policy/route/context/evaluator revisions. It shall randomize paired order where feasible or predeclare constrained assignment and its inferential limits, reset mutable environments and disclose warm caches/native history. Repository/task-family leakage and repeated-task dependence shall be modeled in the registered sampling unit. Prospective `TestExperimentAssignmentPrecedesResults` retains fabricated probabilities on deterministic selection, a post-result feature and duplicate tasks counted as independent.
- **AC-4.2** [I07/I09/I20/I23, AT-28/AT-29, G14] When an assignment is planned, starts or changes disposition, the system shall preserve the registered population, all exclusions with reasons and every started assignment, including failed, blocked, cancelled, exhausted, timed-out, rescued, unverified and unfinished work. Outcome labels shall remain distinct from run states; missing values remain unknown, timeouts are censored, and later regressions append linked corrections with observation windows. Prospective `TestExperimentAllStartedDenominator` retains an abandoned start, failed rescue, missing counter and accepted result followed by a delayed regression.
- **AC-4.3** [I09/I20/I22, AT-29, G14] If a holdout leaks, overlap/identification assumptions fail or confounding cannot be resolved, then the system shall mark the affected inference invalid or inconclusive with reasons and retain the incumbent; deterministic observational logs shall not supply absent counterfactuals. Development tasks and contaminated holdouts shall not be silently relabelled as fresh held-out evidence. Prospective `TestExperimentRejectsContaminatedInference` retains task content leaked to a proposer and harder tasks systematically assigned to a stronger route.

### FR-5 — Canonical execution and foreground priority (v2 §§10.3, 12.3)

- **AC-5.1** [I05/I12/I18/I21/I23/I24, AT-06/AT-08/AT-20/AT-26, G05] When an experimental assignment executes, the system shall use existing canonical admission/run/attempt, owner, workspace, reservation and stop/recovery machinery; it shall not launch an independent executor or replay a native call on receipt/event recovery. P3 shall remain blocked pending MH-14's exact parallel isolation, resource and integration contracts, without blocking sequential preparation. Prospective `TestExperimentCanonicalOwnership` retains duplicate launch after reconnect and a parallel variant with no isolated workspace.
- **AC-5.2** [I06/I10/I12/I21/I24, AT-08/AT-26, G05] When foreground demand, resource pressure, cancellation or budget exhaustion occurs, the system shall fence queued optional dispatch, request normal stop and reconcile active effects before releasing reservations or reassigning capacity. Foreground critical-path verification/repair/completion reserves shall never be borrowed; an unconfirmed stop or expired lease shall retain reservation/quarantine and report uncertainty. Prospective `TestExperimentPreemptionReconciles` retains foreground pressure during an approval wait and a timed-out stop while a child still writes.

### FR-6 — Complete costs and finite stopping (v2 §§7.3, 10.3, 13.1–13.2)

- **AC-6.1** [I09/I10/I21, AT-13/AT-26/AT-28, G05/G14] When producing a receipt, the system shall separately report planning, queueing, native work, retrieval, handoff, verification, review, integration, repair, proposal/summary/evaluation overhead, human effort and delayed-defect observation. Wall time, allowance/token categories and observed monetary charges shall remain separate with source/confidence/missingness; estimates shall not become charges or provider-enforced caps. Prospective `TestExperimentLifecycleCosts` retains overlapping usage counters, unknown native tokens and parallel speedup consuming more allowance.
- **AC-6.2** [I09/I20/I21/I22, AT-26/AT-29, G14] When a registered stop/budget condition is reached, the system shall stop new optional effects within its envelope, reconcile work and retain evidence, even if the current result is unfavorable. Repeated/adaptive comparisons shall use the predeclared correction or a fresh final holdout; peeking or rerunning until favorable shall invalidate the claimed inference. Prospective `TestExperimentStoppingCannotCherryPick` retains repeated unregistered interim checks and an exhausted unfavorable trial submitted for a replacement run.

### FR-7 — Recommendations and truthful inference (v2 §§12.3, 13.2–13.3)

- **AC-7.1** [I07/I09/I20/I22, AT-29/AT-30/AT-33, G14] When reporting results, the system shall show paired effects/distributions, stated uncertainty/assumptions, independent-unit count, missingness/censoring, observation window, quality floor, useful-gain threshold and all budget/authority violations. Insufficient power, sparse/contradictory evidence or an open defect window shall yield inconclusive; a safety/authority/protected-check breach shall reject the challenger before performance ranking. Absence of detected harm shall not prove the quality floor. Prospective `TestExperimentConclusionsRespectEvidence` retains zero observed defects with inadequate sample size and a faster authority-violating challenger.
- **AC-7.2** [I03/I20/I22/I25, AT-29/AT-33/AT-34 boundary, G14] When a valid result supports a proposal, the system shall present a scoped recommendation with provenance, rejected alternatives and limitations while retaining fixed incumbent and active pins. It shall perform no policy-pointer update, automatic promotion, canary, spending/security change or executable update; those require MH-28's reviewed contracts. A losing challenger shall remain recorded as losing. Prospective `TestExperimentRecommendationCannotPromote` retains a development-only win and a favorable report attempting to change the active policy.
- **AC-7.3** [I09/I14/I22, G14] When exporting evidence or proposing an advantage claim, the system shall distinguish documented mechanisms, routine observational delivery, synthetic offline fixtures, separately authorized matched live results and independent release review. Routine private ledger data and passing offline fault cases shall never establish benchmark advantage or all G14 readiness. Prospective `TestExperimentEvidenceClasses` retains a routine delivery average relabelled as causal productivity improvement and a synthetic pass relabelled live.

### FR-8 — Revocation, retention and optional failure (v2 §§5.3, 8.4, 12.2–12.3)

- **AC-8.1** [I06/I12/I20/I25, AT-27/AT-43] When experiment authority or applicable consent is revoked, the system shall fence future experimental assignment/optional writes, cancel queued experiments and stop/reconcile active work before releasing resources. It shall participate in MH-25's scoped preview/export/erasure contract through exact source/consent/protocol/derivation roots; revocation alone shall not claim erasure of essential receipts, backups or provider copies. Prospective `TestExperimentRevocationAndRetention` retains a queued assignment racing revocation and a derived report retaining erased source data.
- **AC-8.2** [I13/I22/I25, AT-33] While experimentation is disabled, empty, unavailable or corrupt, the system shall preserve eligible fixed-policy execution, essential receipts, independent acceptance and recovery with a truthful optional-failure diagnostic and zero hidden calls. Prospective `TestExperimentFailurePreservesFixedWorkflow` retains a corrupt optional store and a hidden proposal-generator call detected by a call sentinel.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **NFR-1** [I13/I21, v2 §§4.3, 10.3, 13.2] Proposed initial offline slice caps: one active local experiment; two variants; at most 10 paired task assignments/20 total starts; one attempt per variant/task and zero experimental repair/replan retries; 60 seconds per scripted attempt and 30 minutes total experiment wall time. Production foreground repairs remain independently reserved. A smaller inherited cap wins; larger cohorts require a prospectively reviewed revision rather than silent widening. These are bounded fixture limits, not a statistical sufficiency claim or live provider cap. Prospective `TestExperimentExecutionBounds` retains exact-limit and one-over-limit inputs plus a hung evaluator; inadequacy shall yield inconclusive.
- **NFR-2** [I13/I21/I23, v2 §§4.3, 17.2] Registration is at most 1 MiB/depth 64; each assignment/outcome record at most 64 KiB; one analysis reads at most 16 MiB and 10,000 linked observations, buffers at most 32 MiB application data, emits at most 1 MiB and has a 30-second deadline. At a bound, reject with explicit incomplete coverage and preserve the all-started denominator; never silently truncate evidence. Prospective `TestExperimentRecordAnalysisBounds` retains each exact-bound/one-over input and cancellation during scan. Limits are proposed, not assertions about current code.
- **NFR-3** [I13/I15/I21/I25, AT-03/AT-26/AT-33] Credential-free offline fixture runs shall make zero native/model/network calls. Exploration defaults to zero allowance, attempts and monetary authority; any later live protocol must freeze a separately authorized finite allowance envelope accounting for in-flight exposure and every auxiliary, plus reconciled stop bounds inherited from qualified producers. Missing envelope or stop evidence blocks live execution. Prospective `TestOfflineExperimentHasNoExternalEffects` uses a failing call sentinel and an undeclared auxiliary.
- **NFR-4** [I19/I20/I23/I25, v2 §§5.3, 8.4] Optional experiment evidence shall use repository-scoped roots/ACLs and MH-25 retention, with derived optional examples/reports expiring within 90 days or a shorter authorized policy; active/pinned essential dependencies require explicit retention explanation. Raw source/task text, prompts, keystrokes, account identifiers and secrets remain excluded by default; cross-project cohorts and telemetry require separate consent. Local permissions shall be 0700 directories/0600 files where supported, without claiming same-user hostile-worker isolation. Prospective `TestExperimentPrivacyScopes` retains cross-repository metadata lookup, raw prompt capture and unhandled backup copies.
- **NFR-5** [I20/I23, v2 §§4–5] Design shall preserve canonical authority, additive compatibility and immutable revisions, distinguish payload schema 2 from actual database and plugin/release versions, inventory migrations afresh and define atomic registration/assignment/consent fencing and crash recovery against approved producers. No API signature or migration number is chosen by this capture. Prospective `TestExperimentCrashRecovery` retains a crash between registered assignment and dispatch and a stale consent revision; neither may duplicate execution.
