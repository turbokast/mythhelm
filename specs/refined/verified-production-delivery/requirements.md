## Verified production delivery — Requirements

> Refined after one fresh-context assessment; Ready for design. Destination-specific live enablement remains separately gated.

> Optional delivery of an exact accepted build to an explicitly authorised, separately qualified destination, with independent health evidence and reconciled effects. Normative source: `mythhelm-synthesis/MYTHHELM_Master_Spec_v2.md`, version 2.0, §§6.3–6.4, 8, 11, 17–18; implementation sequence: `mythhelm-synthesis/MYTHHELM_Implementation_Plan.md` W15. All invariant, gate and acceptance-case references below are version-qualified v2 references, not historical Revision 1.1 claims.

## Context

- **Backlog card**: MH-30
- **Issue**: [#136](https://github.com/turbokast/mythhelm/issues/136), read with `gh issue view 136 --json number,title,body,labels,comments,url`.
- **Capture baseline**: `6d93bbb`; source inspection frozen on 2026-11-03 at 14:36:41 UTC. These observations must be re-grounded before design against the then-current implementation.

MH-30 asks for a completed production outcome that means more than a successful upload. A verified source candidate, the build produced from it, a destination's actual effect and that destination's independently checked health are distinct evidence. A deployment result must preserve their exact identities and uncertainty rather than turn native completion, CI green or a provider exit code into production acceptance.

The feature is optional and disabled by default. Preparing a release may complete a preparation-only request; a request to deploy without applicable authority must preserve the reviewable verified release-ready candidate and report the blocked deployment. Apply, push, PR publication, package publication and deployment each retain their own authority boundary. This requirements capture selects no provider and authorises no live deployment, provisioning, native invocation, account action or paid call.

### Objectives

- **O1**: Preserve a useful, independently verified release-ready build when deployment is disabled or blocked.
- **O2**: Make production completion mean exact build/destination health evidence under applicable authority.
- **O3**: Survive uncertain acknowledgements, restart and failed health/rollback without duplicating effects or hiding unresolved state.

## Functional Requirements

All named checks below are prospective, not existing test names or passed gates. Each check must retain the stated violating input, run a nonempty success/control path and observe actual fake destination calls plus canonical state/evidence, rather than only a rendered label.

### FR-1 — Disabled deployment and useful preparation

- **AC-1.1** [I02/I03 (v2 §2); AT-44 (v2 §18.2); G07/G15 (v2 §18.3)] When a production delivery is requested with deployment disabled or without applicable deployment authority, the system shall preserve the verified release-ready build/manifest, record the missing-authority blocker and keep the requested deployment/run blocked without invoking the destination. `TestReleaseMissingAuthorityPreservesCandidate` shall retain absent, expired and revoked grant inputs and a deliberately invoked fake effect that the assertion rejects; its enabled/valid-grant control shall reach the fake destination.
- **AC-1.2** [I07/I09 (v2 §2); v2 §11.3; AT-44 (v2 §18.2)] When the admitted request is preparation only, the system shall permit completion under its exact preparation acceptance contract with release state `release_ready` and explicitly absent deployment/production-health evidence. `TestPrepareOnlyOutcome` shall retain a forged production-complete result and a failed-build input; neither shall receive verified release-ready or production-complete status.

### FR-2 — Exact build, contract and destination binding

- **AC-2.1** [I07/I20 (v2 §2); v2 §§11.1–11.3; AT-45 (v2 §18.2)] When a release becomes `release_ready`, the system shall bind the immutable build digest and artifact manifest to the exact accepted integrated source/dependency/contract/check/toolchain/environment identities and protected build evidence. Native completion or a mutable branch/path shall not supply that binding. `TestReleaseBuildBinding` shall retain stale source, substituted build bytes, changed check definitions and missing build evidence, each rejected alongside a matching positive build.
- **AC-2.2** [I02/I04/I20 (v2 §2); AT-12/AT-45 (v2 §18.2)] If build, destination, environment, expected target state or required qualification materially changes after preparation, then the system shall invalidate affected authority/evidence and require re-admission before a deployment effect. `TestReleaseBindingDrift` shall retain one changed input for each dimension and assert no changed effect reaches the destination; the unchanged control shall proceed.

### FR-3 — Separate, bounded authority at execution

- **AC-3.1** [I02/I03 (v2 §2); v2 §8.2; AT-12 (v2 §18.2); G07 (v2 §18.3)] When deployment is admitted and again immediately before its external execution, the system shall validate issuer, action class, repository/host/environment, exact artifact or explicitly bounded future-build policy, tool/argv scope, destination, expected state, resource/monetary envelope, expiry, revocation and repetition constraints. `TestDeployGrantScope` shall retain a mismatch/missing mandatory item for each bound dimension, including revocation between intent and execution, and reject all of them while accepting the exact eligible grant.
- **AC-3.2** [I03/I11/I22 (v2 §2); v2 §§8.2–8.3, 11.3; AT-12 (v2 §18.2)] If authority for apply, push, PR publication or package publication is supplied, or repository/model/plugin/learner text requests broader effects, then the system shall not treat it as deployment or rollback authority. `TestEffectAuthoritySeparation` shall retain each such input plus a valid deployment-only grant; the latter shall permit its deployment but refuse an ungranted rollback or publication.

### FR-4 — Canonical effect lifecycle and reconciliation

- **AC-4.1** [I12/I23 (v2 §2); v2 §§6.3, 11.3; AT-09 (v2 §18.2); G15 (v2 §18.3)] When a deployment executes, the canonical controller shall durably record its unique intent, exact build/destination/expected state/grant and destination idempotency or lookup identity before sending, then retain observation and reconciliation evidence linked to the run and Release. `TestDeployIntentBeforeEffect` shall inject persistence failure before intent and crashes after intent/send/response/persistence; the retained missing-intent-send case shall be rejected, and recovery shall produce at most one logical effect for the same operation identity.
- **AC-4.2** [I09/I12 (v2 §2); AT-09/AT-45 (v2 §18.2)] If an acknowledgement is lost or execution is ambiguous, then the system shall record `external_effect_uncertain`, keep the Release `blocked`, and look up/reconcile the exact effect before permitting further execution. Without reliable lookup or idempotency establishing safety, it shall require manual resolution. `TestDeployLostAcknowledgement` shall retain success-with-response-loss, definite-not-executed and unreliable-lookup destinations; a blind resend after successful effect shall fail the call-count assertion.
- **AC-4.3** [I06/I12/I18/I23 (v2 §2); v2 §§6.4, 11.3; AT-45 (v2 §18.2)] When a client reconnects, the controller restarts, or concurrent clients request the same release effect, the system shall reconcile the canonical operation and preserve its bounds without launching another effect from replay, client retry or a lease timeout. `TestReleaseReconnectOwnership` shall retain replayed events, duplicate concurrent requests and ambiguous old ownership; its intentionally duplicated-send fixture shall be rejected.

### FR-5 — Independent destination health and truthful completion

- **AC-5.1** [I07/I09 (v2 §2); v2 §11.3; AT-45 (v2 §18.2); G15 (v2 §18.3)] When deployment returns success, the Release shall enter `deployed_unverified` until protected independent checks establish the exact deployed build and destination's required health under the pinned health contract/environment. `TestDeploySuccessNeedsIndependentHealth` shall retain a zero-exit upload with failed/missing health, healthy old build and healthy different destination; all shall fail production acceptance, while the matching healthy build shall enter `deployed_healthy`.
- **AC-5.2** [I07/I20 (v2 §2); v2 §§8.3, 11.2–11.3; AT-45 (v2 §18.2)] If the health evaluator/definitions change, or the candidate/provider asserts its own health, then the system shall refuse to reuse that assertion as independent evidence and preserve an explicit unavailable/invalid/stale reason. `TestHealthEvidenceProtectedBinding` shall retain candidate-edited check definitions, provider-only success and changed evaluator identity, contrasted with evidence from the protected pinned evaluator.
- **AC-5.3** [I09 (v2 §2); v2 §11.3; AT-44/AT-45 (v2 §18.2)] When the requested outcome is production delivery, the run shall reach `completed` only with its exact destination/build's accepted independent health evidence; failed, unavailable or uncertain health shall retain the candidate and the observed deployment disposition. `TestProductionOutcomeMapping` shall retain one input for every Release state and reject a mapping that treats `release_ready`, `deployed_unverified`, `blocked`, `rolled_back` or a provider exit alone as successful requested production delivery.

### FR-6 — Bounded, authorised rollback

- **AC-6.1** [I02/I03/I21 (v2 §2); v2 §§8.2, 11.3; AT-12/AT-45 (v2 §18.2)] If protected health verification fails, then the system shall enter `rollback_pending` only when an applicable rollback grant covers the preregistered failure condition, exact destination/current state, prior verified build or defined restoration target, deadline, repetition and resource envelope; otherwise it shall remain blocked with retained evidence. `TestRollbackConditionAndScope` shall retain missing rollback authority, expired authority, unmet trigger, changed prior build and conflicting destination state, with a matching authorised failure as positive control.
- **AC-6.2** [I09/I12 (v2 §2); v2 §11.3; AT-09/AT-45 (v2 §18.2)] When rollback executes, the system shall journal it as a distinct effect and independently verify the restored destination before claiming `rolled_back`; definite failure shall record `rollback_failed`, while uncertainty shall remain blocked for reconciliation without blind retry. `TestRollbackFailureAndUncertainty` shall retain lost rollback acknowledgement, failed restore, failed restore-health and unreliable lookup, and reject a false rolled-back result or second uncertain restore call.

### FR-7 — Qualification, credentials and one controller

- **AC-7.1** [I02/I14/I23 (v2 §2); v2 §18.1; G07/G15 (v2 §18.3)] When destination support is enabled, the system shall require versioned per-destination evidence for identity, authority enforcement, expected-state races, idempotency/lookup, independent health and supported rollback limitations; missing evidence shall block automatic production effects. `TestDestinationQualificationGate` shall retain missing, stale and unrelated-destination qualification, with a complete synthetic qualification control. Fake results shall be labelled offline and shall not qualify any real destination.
- **AC-7.2** [I03/I19 (v2 §2); v2 §§8.3, 11.3; G07 (v2 §18.3)] When ordinary build/test/native execution runs, production credentials shall remain unavailable to it; destination execution shall resolve only scoped opaque secret references within its qualified release boundary. `TestReleaseCredentialSeparation` shall use synthetic marker secrets to detect their presence in worker/check environment, argv, persisted receipts and exports, retaining a deliberately leaked marker case that fails; no native subscription secret extraction shall be introduced.
- **AC-7.3** [I22/I23/I25 (v2 §2); v2 §§11.3, 18.1] When learning is disabled and a fixed qualified route plus valid release authority exist, delivery shall use the canonical run/effect controller and remain operable without an S4/S5 dependency or separate deployment controller. `TestReleaseWithoutLearning` shall exercise the full fake preparation/deploy/health path with learning off and retain an artificial learning prerequisite/second effect owner that the check rejects.

### FR-8 — Receipts, bounded operations and failure visibility

- **AC-8.1** [I09/I20/I23 (v2 §2); v2 §17.2; AT-45 (v2 §18.2)] When any release transition or reconciliation occurs, the receipt and inspection view shall expose linked run/release/effect identities, build/destination, authority reference, expected/observed state, evidence provenance, health/rollback results, unresolved blockers and consumed/remaining bounds, distinguishing observed/reported/unknown data. `TestReleaseReceiptStates` shall retain absent evidence represented falsely as zero/passed, mismatched release/run references and each failure state; the canonical matching results shall render without inventing success.
- **AC-8.2** [I12/I21 (v2 §2); v2 §§6.4, 17.2] If persistence fails, a deadline expires, cancellation is requested or a cap is exhausted, then the system shall stop starting dependent effects, preserve the candidate and unresolved ownership/effect evidence, and report unconfirmed cancellation until reconciliation establishes the outcome. `TestReleaseBoundsAndPersistenceFailure` shall retain a destination that succeeds after timeout and a journal-write failure after send; neither may release ownership, report no effect, or trigger an unbounded retry.

## Non-Functional Requirements

These are initial bounded defaults for refinement, not measured performance or provider hard-cap claims. A destination may need stricter limits; widening any default requires an explicit reviewed contract revision and applicable authority.

- **NFR-1** [I10/I21 (v2 §2); v2 §17.2]: One active mutating release effect per destination/environment, at most 16 queued release requests per run, and a maximum 30-minute release episode including health/reconciliation/rollback. Queue overflow is an explicit refusal preserving existing evidence. `TestReleaseEnvelopeLimits` shall retain limit-plus-one inputs and an elapsed deadline.
- **NFR-2** [I12/I21 (v2 §2); v2 §6.4]: Each destination call has a timeout of at most 30 seconds; health verification has at most 10 probes within 5 minutes; transient transport recovery has at most three retries per operation, with mutating retries allowed only after established idempotency or reconciliation. After one unresolved reconciliation pass, automatic work stops until fresh evidence or an authorised decision. At most one rollback effect is admitted per release episode. `TestReleaseRetryAndHealthCaps` shall retain a fourth retry, eleventh probe and second rollback and observe their refusal; reconnect shall not reset counters/deadlines.
- **NFR-3** [I09/I21 (v2 §2); v2 §17.2]: Each normalized destination/health response retained inline is at most 64 KiB; each release receipt/manifest is at most 1 MiB, with at most 32 MiB of linked evidence per release episode. Oversize data must be rejected or explicitly truncated with original size/digest where obtainable; truncation cannot supply a passed check. Evidence-limit exhaustion blocks new effects without deleting active/ambiguous evidence. `TestReleaseEvidenceCaps` shall retain boundary and boundary-plus-one responses/receipts and an exhausted evidence budget.
- **NFR-4** [I13/I14 (v2 §2); G15 (v2 §18.3)]: Contributor acceptance shall use deterministic offline fake destinations, a fake clock and synthetic secrets with zero production credentials, paid calls or provider account operations. Every enabled real destination needs separately authorised live evidence for response loss, health failure, expiry/revocation, stale build, rollback failure and reconnect. `TestReleaseOfflineSuiteHasNoExternalAccess` shall retain an attempted external connection and reject it; the fake suite shall still complete its positive effect path.

- **OQ-3 — Qualified health and rollback semantics.** Blocks per-destination production claims.
