# 0001. License and contribution policy

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-29

## Context

MYTHHELM must be public, open source and entirely free, and broadly adoptable by individuals and companies. Contributions will include AI-assisted work.

## Decision

- License the official core, adapters, SDK, documentation and original themes under **Apache-2.0**, which is permissive and includes an express patent grant.
- Accept contributions under the **Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1**, enforced by CI. There is no CLA and no copyright assignment.
- Accept AI-assisted contributions when a human contributor signs off, attesting that they reviewed the change and have the right to submit it.

## Consequences

Others may fork and charge for derivatives. Keeping the official project free is a governance commitment, not a license restriction. Third-party code and assets keep their own notices, and dependency license compatibility is checked in CI.
